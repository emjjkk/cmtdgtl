Smart Living & Intelligent Spaces: Home & Commercial Automation

Welcome to the future of modern space management. At Comet Digital, we transform residential and commercial properties across Rwanda with cutting-edge, integrated automation systems. Whether you are upgrading an exclusive private villa, modernizing a high-end apartment, or equipping a commercial venue with centralized control, our tailored automation solutions deliver unmatched convenience, enhanced security, and optimized energy efficiency.

What we do

Total control, locally or remotely.

We integrate smart hardware, Room Control Units (RCUs), intuitive mobile apps, and central management software to put total control of your environment at your fingertips—locally or remotely.

  • Smart Lighting & Scene Control: Dimmable, scheduled, and mood-based lighting routines designed to transform your space with a single tap or voice command.
  • Climate & HVAC Management: Intelligent thermostats and RCU-driven climate systems that maintain ideal comfort while minimizing energy waste.
  • App-Controlled Security & Access: Integrated electronic locks, smart door stations, real-time surveillance, and automated perimeter control accessible anytime via your smartphone.
  • Automated Window Blinds & Curtains: Motorized shade systems synchronized with natural daylight cycles to protect interiors and reduce heat load.
  • Distributed Multi-Zone Audio & Entertainment: Concealed background music systems and multi-room audio zones tailored for ambient relaxation or immersive entertaining.

Tailored solutions

Tailored Solutions for Every Property Scale

Whether you are designing a high-end personal residence or specifying automated infrastructure for a boutique commercial venue, we design solutions scaled precisely to your operational needs.

1. Residential Automation Solutions

Transform your house into an intuitive, responsive home that adapts to your everyday lifestyle.

Medium-Scale Residential (Modern Apartments & Townhomes):

  • Essential smart lighting scenes (Welcome, Movie, Away).
  • Smart keyless entry (biometric, keypad, and mobile app unlocking).
  • Automated climate scheduling and smart AC integration.
  • Centralized control via a sleek wall panel or smartphone app.

Exclusive & Luxury Private Residences (Villas & Estates):

  • Full-property integration combining lighting, climate, motorized curtains, and surround audio.
  • Advanced occupancy sensing and custom automated routines (e.g., automated evening illumination and security arming).
  • Comprehensive app control with secure off-site remote access.
  • Custom architectural keypad finishes and flush-mount touch panels tailored to your interior design.

2. Commercial & Hospitality Automation

Streamline property management, lower operational overhead, and impress guests with intelligent commercial infrastructure.

Medium-Scale Commercial (Boutique Offices, Retail Stores, & Cafes):

  • Automated lighting and HVAC scheduling to eliminate off-hours energy waste.
  • Centralized app management for staff access and security monitoring.
  • Background music zone control to set the perfect atmosphere for customers.
  • Smart meeting room automation (one-touch presentation modes for display, lighting, and blinds).

Exclusive Commercial & Luxury Hospitality (Hotels, VIP Lounges, & Executive Suites):

  • Dedicated Room Control Unit (RCU) architecture for guest room management (smart doors, scene lighting, background audio, and curtain control).
  • Centralized management server software for real-time energy monitoring, room status tracking, and preventative maintenance alerts.
  • Seamless integration with access control, smart keycards, and property management systems.
  • Premium guest touch panels and energy-saving master room controls.
