In-house software

Powered by Our Proprietary In-House Software

Say goodbye to outdated static menus and complex third-party software. Our proprietary digital signage platform gives you full cloud-based control over your entire network of displays from any device.

Key Software Capabilities

Instant Real-Time Updates: Change prices, menu items, sold-out items, or promotional banners across single or multiple locations in seconds.
Automated Dayparting & Scheduling: Automatically transition menus based on the time of day—seamlessly switching from breakfast to lunch, dinner, or late-night happy hour.
Multi-Zone Layout Templates: Divide screen real estate into dynamic zones to show live menu items, high-definition promotional videos, scrolling ticker notices, or social media feeds simultaneously.
Centralized Multi-Location Management: Manage menu displays across a single venue or synchronize an entire restaurant chain across multiple branches from a single dashboard.
Offline Mode Reliability: Keeps your menus playing smoothly without interruption, even during occasional local network outages.
