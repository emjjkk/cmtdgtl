About us

Built to help local businesses compete with confidence.

Established in 2019, Comet Digital Limited was founded with a mission to empower Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs) across Kigali by providing accessible, high-quality digital solutions. Since our inception, we have dedicated ourselves to helping local businesses bridge the gap between traditional operations and the modern digital landscape.

By combining technical expertise with a deep understanding of the Rwandan market, we enable our clients to enhance their brand presence and streamline customer interactions through reliable and innovative technology. From local eateries to established hotels, we partner with our clients to transform physical spaces into engaging digital environments that keep Kigali’s business community vibrant, modern, and tech-driven.
